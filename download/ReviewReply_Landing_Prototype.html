FAQ

Questions, answered

How does the AI brand voice training work?
We ingest your last 200–500 published replies (or a writing sample) and Claude 3.5 fine-tunes a private voice profile stored encrypted at rest. The profile captures tone, signature phrases, response length, and emoji conventions. We retrain weekly as new replies are approved, so the voice evolves with your business.
Which review sources are supported?
Google Business Profile (official API), Facebook Pages (Graph API), Yelp (partnership API), Trustpilot (Business API), Apple Maps (RSS), and Bing Places (RSS). Reviews from all sources land in a single unified inbox within ~5 minutes of being posted publicly.
Is ReviewReply TCPA-compliant for SMS?
Yes. We capture explicit opt-in at point-of-sale or booking, enforce quiet hours (9pm–8am local), register all numbers via 10DLC campaigns, honor STOP/UNSTOP keywords within 60 seconds, and timestamp every consent event for audit. Full TCPA attestation available on Enterprise.
Can I use ReviewReply if I'm an agency?
Yes — the Enterprise tier includes full agency mode: white-label domain & branding, a client-facing portal with per-client login, per-seat pricing (so you only pay for active clients), and role-based access control for your account managers. Most agencies resell at $299–$499/client with 80%+ margins.
How long is the free trial?
14 days, no credit card required. You get full Pro features unlocked, including brand voice training and competitor intelligence. Your data (reviews imported, replies drafted) is retained for 30 days after trial ends in case you upgrade — then permanently deleted.
Do you offer SSO for enterprise?
Yes — both SAML 2.0 and OIDC are supported on Enterprise. Tested IdPs: Okta, Microsoft Entra ID (Azure AD), Google Workspace, and OneLogin. SCIM provisioning is in beta. Setup takes ~20 minutes with our provisioning guide; our team will join the call if you prefer.
What's your SOC2 status?
SOC2 Type I attestation is in progress with AICPA-affiliated auditors; Type II continuous monitoring begins at general availability. The full report is available to Enterprise customers under NDA via our Trust Center. We also publish a real-time status page and sub-processors list.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes — no contracts, no cancellation fees. Cancel from the billing portal in two clicks. Monthly plans end at the next billing cycle; annual plans receive a pro-rated refund for unused months. Your data exports (CSV of all reviews, replies, and analytics) remain downloadable for 90 days post-cancellation.
