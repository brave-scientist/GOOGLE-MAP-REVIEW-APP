### 2. PRE-REMEDIATION FINDING

**PARTIALLY IMPLEMENTED (with UI stubs and disconnected business context)**

Prior to remediation:
- **Backend persistence**: The Prisma schema included `Competitor` and `CompetitorSnapshot` models, and `/api/competitors` implemented CRUD endpoints with basic snapshot history tracking.
- **Deficiencies Identified**:
  1. `src/app/competitors/page.tsx` rendered a static mock alert banner: *"Golden Dragon is running a review campaign"* with hardcoded velocity numbers regardless of active business or whether any competitors exist.
  2. The page displayed three static mock cards under *"AI Strategy Suggestions"* claiming to be *"Generated by AI · based on competitor analysis"*, hardcoded to Golden Dragon, fictional restroom cleanliness, and wait times.
  3. The page contained the disclaimer: *"Showing demo data · Real competitor sync (Google Places API) is on our roadmap"*.
  4. The page was completely disconnected from `useActiveBusiness()`, meaning multi-location switching in the sidebar did not update competitor data, and newly added competitors were not scoped to the active location.
  5. The "You" row used hardcoded fallbacks (4.6 rating, 150 reviews, 0.2 trend, 85% response rate, 0.72 sentiment score) with hardcoded labels (*"25% above market"*, *"below market avg"*).
  6. Empty states were missing or non-functional, continuing to show fake mock cards when 0 competitors were tracked.

---

### 3. ROOT CAUSE

When Stage 2 Milestone 2A introduced the `Competitor` and `CompetitorSnapshot` persistence models and `/api/competitors` route, the frontend in `src/app/competitors/page.tsx` was only partially hooked up:
- The initial prototype's hardcoded JSX cards (Golden Dragon alert, demo disclaimer, fake AI strategy suggestions) were left intact.
- The page relied on `dashData?.businesses?.[0]` instead of the application's standard `useActiveBusiness()` context hook.
- No dynamic analysis engine was built on the backend to compare the active business's actual review performance against competitor snapshots, leading developers to leave placeholder text in place.

### 5. SECURITY VERIFICATION

1. **Organization Isolation**:
   - Tenant A (`orgA`) cannot read, modify, or delete competitors belonging to Tenant B (`orgB`).
   - Cross-tenant requests to `GET /api/competitors?businessId=<tenantB_business>` are rejected with **HTTP 403 Forbidden**.
   - Cross-tenant attempts to `PATCH /api/competitors` or `DELETE /api/competitors?id=<tenantB_competitor>` fail closed with **HTTP 404 Not Found**.
2. **Business-Level Scoping**:
   - When Tenant A has multiple locations (Business A1 and Business A2), queries scoped to Business A1 return strictly Business A1 competitors. Competitors from Business A2 do not leak.
   - Client parameter spoofing attempts to supply a foreign `businessId` fail closed via `assertBusinessOwnership()`.
3. **Plan Gating**:
   - `getTenantContext(request, 'PRO')` strictly blocks organizations on FREE or STARTER tiers with **HTTP 403 Forbidden**.
4. **Audit Logging**:
   - Every mutation (`competitor.created`, `competitor.updated`, `competitor.deleted`) creates a persistent `AuditLog` record containing `actorId`, `targetId`, and `metadata`.
